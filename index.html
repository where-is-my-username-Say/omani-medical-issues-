What is traditional medicine?

Traditional medicine is an important part of Omani cultural heritage. For generations, people have relied on local plants, natural materials, and traditional knowledge to care for their health. Today, these practices can be explored through scientific research and modern healthcare.

      Evidence levels used on this site
